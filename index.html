About Kodiyattil Family

The Kodiyattil family is a close-knit Christian family originating from Kerala, India, with a rich heritage and strong family values.

Our family tree traces back to Pathrose and Mariyam Kodiyattil, who established our family legacy in the early 20th century.

Today, the Kodiyattil family spans multiple generations with members across India and abroad, maintaining our traditions while embracing modern values.

This portal serves as a digital home for our family history, connections, and shared memories.
